# Agenda & Recap: Style Variations

Three ways the same emails could look. The meeting and the content are identical in each one, so only the style changes.

Every version keeps the required pieces:
- **Agenda:** end-state (max 3), agenda items (max 6), "Anything I miss?"
- **Recap:** decisions (max 6), action items by person (max 6), "Anything I miss?"

| | A. Clean & Plain | B. Visual Headers | C. Ultra-Short |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Feel** | Professional, classic | Friendly, easy to scan | Fastest read, mobile-first |
| **Best for** | Execs, formal accounts | Most day-to-day customers | Quick weekly syncs |
| **Watch out** | Can blend in with other emails | Emojis may feel too casual for some | Inline lists get long past ~4 items |

---

## Variation A: Clean & Plain

No emojis or formatting tricks. Looks right in any inbox.

### Agenda

> **Subject:** Agenda | HubSpot Kickoff | Thu 10am
>
> Hi Sarah,
>
> **Goal: we'll leave with**
> - Agreed scope and go-live date
> - Owners named on both sides
> - System access sorted
>
> **Agenda**
> 1. Intros + roles
> 2. Your top 3 goals
> 3. Scope: in / out
> 4. Timeline + milestones
> 5. Access checklist
> 6. Next steps
>
> Anything I miss?
>
> Manuel

### Recap

> **Subject:** Recap | HubSpot Kickoff
>
> Hi Sarah, thanks for the time today.
>
> **Decisions**
> - Go-live: Dec 15
> - Phase 1 = Sales Hub only
> - Sarah signs off for your team
> - Weekly sync: Thursdays, 30 min
>
> **Next steps**
>
> *Sarah*
> - Grant HubSpot admin access (Fri)
> - Share Salesforce field list (Mon)
>
> *Manuel*
> - Send project plan (Tue)
> - Send Thursday invite (today)
>
> Anything I miss?
>
> Manuel

## Variation C: Ultra-Short

The subject line carries the main point. Short lists go on one line, and action items look like a to-do list. Readable in about 10 seconds on a phone.

### Agenda

> **Subject:** Kickoff Thu 10am: agenda inside
>
> Sarah, here's the plan for Thursday.
>
> **Walk out with:** scope + go-live date · owners · system access
>
> **Agenda**
> 1. Intros
> 2. Goals
> 3. Scope
> 4. Timeline
> 5. Access
> 6. Next steps
>
> Anything I miss?
>
> Manuel

### Recap

> **Subject:** Kickoff recap: go-live Dec 15
>
> Sarah, quick recap:
>
> **Decided:** Dec 15 go-live · Sales Hub first · Sarah signs off · Thursday syncs
>
> **To-dos**
> - ☐ Sarah: HubSpot admin access · Fri
> - ☐ Sarah: Salesforce field list · Mon
> - ☐ Manuel: Project plan · Tue
> - ☐ Manuel: Thursday invite · today
>
> Anything I miss?
>
> Manuel

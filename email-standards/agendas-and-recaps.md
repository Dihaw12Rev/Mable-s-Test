# Customer Email Standard: Agendas & Recaps

Short and easy to scan. A customer should be able to read one of these in under 30 seconds.

Want to compare looks? See [style-variations.md](style-variations.md) for three ways to style the same emails.

## The rules

| | Agenda | Recap |
|---|---|---|
| **Section 1** | 🎯 End-state: what we'll walk out with (max 3) | ✅ Decisions made (max 6) |
| **Section 2** | 📋 Agenda items to get there (max 6) | 📌 Action items, grouped by person (max 6 total) |
| **Close** | "Anything I miss?" | "Anything I miss?" |

**Do:**
- Keep each bullet to one line, about 10 words or fewer.
- Start each action item with a verb and give it a due date.
- Use a Loom when something needs more than a bullet to explain.

**Don't:**
- Paste AI-generated discussion notes without cutting them down.
- Write paragraphs or long blocks of text.
- Add a "Discussion" or "Background" section. If it's important, it's a decision or an action item. If it isn't, cut it.

## Agenda examples

### Agenda 1: Onboarding kickoff

> **Subject:** Agenda: HubSpot Kickoff, Thu 10am
>
> Hi Sarah,
>
> **🎯 We'll walk out with:**
> - Agreed project scope and go-live date
> - Named owners on both sides
> - Access to your HubSpot + Salesforce
>
> **📋 Agenda:**
> 1. Intros + roles (5 min)

> 3. Scope review: what's in / out
> 4. Timeline + milestones
> 5. System access checklist
> 6. Next steps

>
> Manuel

## Recap examples

### Recap 1: Onboarding kickoff

> **Subject:** Recap: HubSpot Kickoff
>
> Hi Sarah,
>
> **✅ Decisions:**
> - Go-live: Dec 15
> - Phase 1 = Sales Hub only; Service Hub moves to Q1
> - Sarah owns sign-off on the customer side
> - Weekly sync: Thursdays, 30 min
>
> **📌 Action items:**
> - **Sarah:** Grant HubSpot super-admin access (Fri)
> - **Sarah:** Share Salesforce field list (Mon)
> - **Manuel:** Send project plan + timeline (Tue)
> - **Manuel:** Set up recurring Thursday invite (today)
>
> Anything I miss?
>
> Manuel

## Before you hit send

- [ ] Agenda: 3 or fewer end-states, 6 or fewer agenda items
- [ ] Recap: 6 or fewer decisions, 6 or fewer action items, each with an owner and a date
- [ ] No paragraphs. Every bullet fits on one line.
- [ ] Anything that needs explaining → Loom link
- [ ] Ends with "Anything I miss?"

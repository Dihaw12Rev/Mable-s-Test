02 · Before the meeting

Prep, in order

About an hour. Each step says where to go in HubSpot Work and what to check before moving on.

01

Paste the fix-up prompt

HubSpot Work › Client Onboarding › chat. Paste hubspot-work-fix-prompt.txt and approve the plan.
  • Onboarding Library shows 58 tasks.
  • Customers has Products purchased, Create tasks and Tasks created for.
  • Agent schedules show Bogotá time.
02

Build the missing agents

HubSpot Work › Agents › New agent. For each, start from the named sample or a blank agent and paste its prompt from hubspot-work-agent-prompts.md.
  • Onboarding Task Generator (sample: Auto-create customer onboarding).
  • Weekly Update Drafter (sample: Weekly stakeholder updates).
  • Request Triage (sample: Auto-route requests).
03

Fill the client's Customers row

Onboarding Portfolio › Customers › the row starting Nov 5.
  • Products purchased = Marketing Hub Onboarding.
  • Program length = 90, Start date = Nov 5, Hub tiers = Marketing Pro.
  • Architect, Company and Deal filled.
  • Progress measures: 0.1, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, with baseline 'to measure in the audit' and a target if Phil has one.
  • Leave Create tasks unticked. You'll tick it in the meeting.
04

Share the workspace

Share on the Client Onboarding workspace.
  • Phil and the Architect can open both projects.
05

Set up the jam board

Duplicate the sales FigJam for this client.
  • Sections: Goal, Progress measures, This week, Decisions, Not now.
  • Link saved in the Customers row's FigJam link.
06

Draft the welcome pieces

Use the drafts in Appendix A.
  • Welcome email, ready to send.
  • "How we work" Supered announcement, ready to post.
Bring to the meeting: the Customer Onboarding SOP, this guide with the decision log (chapter 04) printed or open, and HubSpot Work open on the client's Customers row.
03 · Running the meeting

45 minutes, six blocks

Keep to the blocks. Each one has a goal, what to do on screen, what to check before moving on, and what to do if it goes sideways. Write every decision in the log as you go.

0–5 min

Put the client live

See the tasks appear.

Do

  • Open Onboarding Portfolio › Customers and show the client's row.
  • Tick Create tasks.
  • Open Customer Task › By customer and filter to the client.

Check before moving on

  • 74 tasks appear (Core + Marketing Hub).
  • 4.11 is due Dec 5, 8.05 is due Feb 3, 2027; P0 tasks have no date.
  • Tasks created for = Marketing Hub Onboarding; Create tasks unticked itself.
If it goes sidewaysIf no tasks appear within 15 minutes, move on. Ops copies them by hand after the meeting.
5–15 min

Agree the launch scope

Everyone knows what's real for this client.

Do

  • Walk the three lists: use from day one, by hand for this client, after this client.
  • Move anything that's in the wrong list.

Check before moving on

  • Decision log rows 1–2 filled.
If it goes sidewaysIf something is in neither list, it waits until after this client.
15–25 min

Set the jam rhythm

The calendar for the next 90 days is decided.

Do

  • Pick the weekly jam day, time and length. Suggested: Thursday, 30 minutes, so the Friday update can summarize it.
  • Confirm the kickoff jam is 30 minutes, between Nov 10 and Nov 15.
  • Confirm the MBR jams: Fri Dec 4 (Day 30 is a Saturday) and Mon Jan 4.
  • Agree the rule: every jam action item becomes a task or request in HubSpot Work the same day.

Check before moving on

  • Decision log rows 3–5 filled.
If it goes sidewaysIf the day can't be fixed yet, the Architect proposes it to the client in the welcome email.
25–35 min

Supered: announcements and templates

Supered has clear jobs and an owner.

Do

  • Agree what Supered carries: the Action Plan, the welcome announcement, the Friday update, delivery videos.
  • Ask which HubSpot config templates Supered holds today.
  • Pick the first three templates to map to library tasks (candidates: 4.05 lifecycle stages, 4.10 baseline dashboard, MH-5.02 lead scoring and nurture).

Check before moving on

  • Decision log rows 6–7 filled.
  • Template owner named.
If it goes sidewaysIf no templates exist yet, build in the portal as usual and note which builds should become templates.
35–40 min

Switch the agents on

The automation that helps from day one is running.

Do

  • Resume: Task Generator, Handoff Brief, Delay Flagger, Portfolio Sync, Request Triage, Weekly Update Drafter.
  • Leave What's Next Prep and KPI Snapshot as they are; they don't fire until January and February.

Check before moving on

  • Decision log row 8 filled.
If it goes sidewaysIf one misbehaves later, pause just that one and keep going by hand.
40–45 min

Close out this client's handoff

The Architect can start tomorrow without questions.

Do

  • Confirm the progress measures and where each baseline will come from.
  • List any missing pre-sale items and who fixes them.
  • Confirm who sends the welcome (suggested: the Architect, today).

Check before moving on

  • Decision log rows 9–10 filled.
  • Every row has an owner and a date.
If it goes sidewaysIf a measure can't be agreed, leave it blank with an owner. Decide it at the handoff jam.

"How we work" Supered announcement (Day 0)

Title: How we'll work together

Where things live
- Supered: your Action Plan, our updates and short videos of what we build.
- Our shared board: every call is a working session on the same board, so decisions stay in one place.
- Email: anything urgent.

Our rhythm
- Every {Thursday}: a 30-minute working session.
- Every Friday: a short update here with what we did and why it matters, your numbers against where we started, anything we found, next week's plan, and your to-dos.
- Monthly reviews: around Dec 4 and Jan 4.

Requests
- Small extra requests (about 2 hours of work) are $250 each. We confirm the price before we start.
- If something we built breaks, we fix it within one business day at no charge.
- We reply to every other request within one business day.

AI
- We use AI to draft some updates. A person reviews everything before it reaches you.

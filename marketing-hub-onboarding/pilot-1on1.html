Running the new client on the new way of working

A lean launch for the client starting today: HubSpot Work for the work, FigJam jam sessions as the calls, Supered for everything the client sees. Anything not ready yet is done by hand for this client, then automated.

Day 0 Thu Nov 5Program 90 daysEnds Wed Feb 3, 2027Hub Marketing Pro

"Maybe it's only using HubSpot Work + having jam sessions as the call times + Supered for announcements. And other things won't be ready, like the email cadences."

Proposal

What we use, what we do by hand, what waits

Use from day one

  • HubSpot Work: the client's Customers row, their 74 tasks, requests and update drafts.
  • Jam sessions on one FigJam board as every call: handoff, kickoff, weekly, MBRs, what's next.
  • Supered for the Action Plan, announcements, weekly updates and delivery videos.
  • Agents on (credits are fine): Task Generator, Handoff Brief, Delay Flagger, Portfolio Sync, Request Triage, Weekly Update Drafter.

By hand for this client

  • Welcome emails #1 and #2: one personal email from the Architect, plus a "How we work" Supered announcement.
  • Reminders: the Delay Flagger flags; the Architect chases.
  • Intake form: the questions go out as a Supered to-do, and the rest are covered in the kickoff jam.
  • Supered Action Plan: built for this client instead of from a template.
  • Config work: built in the portal as usual until templates exist.

After this client

  • Email cadences (welcome series, reminders) in HubSpot.
  • Supered "HubSpot config templates" mapped to library tasks.
  • Supered Action Plan template per hub.
  • Intake form.
  • First Sales or Service Hub client on their libraries.
Calls become jam sessions

One FigJam board, every call

Start the client's board from the sales FigJam, so the From→To goal and progress measures are already on it. Sections: Goal, Progress measures, This week, Decisions, Not now. Every action item from a jam becomes a task or request in HubSpot Work the same day.

Supered config templates

Decisions

To settle in the 1:1

  1. Do jam sessions replace bookable office hours completely?SuggestedYes for this client: one weekly jam is easier to run than open slots.
  2. Weekly jam: which day, and is 30 minutes right?SuggestedThursday, so the Friday update can summarize it.
  3. Does the weekly update go out as a Supered announcement instead of an email?SuggestedYes, since email cadences aren't ready. The agent drafts it; the Architect posts it.
  4. Is the kickoff a 15-minute call or the first 30-minute jam?SuggestedA 30-minute jam, since it's the client's first look at the board.
  5. Which Supered config templates exist today, and who keeps them up to date?Needed before we add the "Supered template" column to the library.
  6. Who sends the welcome while there's no email cadence: Phil or the Architect?SuggestedThe Architect, from Phil's draft, so the client meets their main contact from day one.
  7. What are this client's progress measures and baselines?The Customers row has none yet. The Handoff Brief and every weekly update depend on them.

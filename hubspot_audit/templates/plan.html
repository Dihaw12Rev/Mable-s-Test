__PORTAL__ · audit review plan

How to review this audit

Nine phases, ordered so the risk-free work happens first and nothing irreversible happens before there is a backup. Each phase names the exact cohort, where to find it, the rule for deciding, and the mistake to avoid.

__TOTALS__

Work top to bottom. The first two phases are evidence-backed and need no debate — clearing them makes everything after easier to read. Phase 4 carries the real business risk, and it is the only phase where doing nothing is also a risk. Each phase names the workbook tab holding its work list.

__BODY__
Counts are as of __DATE__ and drift as the account changes — re-run the audit before acting on a figure that looks stale. Every number comes from the account’s own settings; anything driven by an outside integration or custom code is invisible to the audit, so treat each footprint as a floor rather than a ceiling.

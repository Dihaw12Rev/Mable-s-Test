Methodology & Access

Read-only scan via the HubSpot API on 2026-07-20, covering 17,492 contacts, 6,128 companies, and 7,037 deals (2,583 open). Scored against RevGravy Assessment Rubric v1.0: each check is Pass (100), Warn (50), or Fail (0), weighted within its pillar; pillar scores are weighted 25/20/20/15/20 into the overall grade. Pillars with zero assessed checks are excluded and the remainder re-weighted — they are never counted as failures. Every Fail/Warn finding below carries a severity score (pillar weight × check weight ÷ 100), a fix-skill slug, and a RevGravy SKU, per the rubric's output contract.

Blocked by connection scopes: forms and marketing email require reauthorization of the HubSpot connection; workflows, seat/login activity, and dashboards are not exposed through the current connector at all. Pending extraction script: duplicate-company grouping, property-usage sprawl, lifecycle regression sampling, list staleness, integration health. The companion Python extractor (private app token) unlocks all remaining checks. [info]-only: UTM discipline (4.5) is diagnostic per the rubric — reported, never scored, regardless of access.

Observed but unscored: in a sample of 100 owner records, roughly 72 are deactivated, with duplicate and blank-name entries. Deal-stage names are duplicated across pipelines. Both are logged as governance flags pending full assessment.

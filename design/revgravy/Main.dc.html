RevGravy · product roadmap

From audit tool to retention product

Retainers don't churn on quality of work. They churn the month a client stops being able to see what they're paying for.

Every phase below exists to make the invisible work visible.

01 ~1 week

Drift Engine

Snapshot the account monthly, diff it, and describe what changed in plain English.

80% already built
New: diff, narration, schedule
Unlocks
The monthly artefact that justifies the retainer
02 ~2 weeks

Write-back

Push the generated description into every workflow's own description field, inside HubSpot.

Text already generated
New: writer, dry-run, revert
Unlocks
Your work lives in their tool, with your name on it
03 ~3 weeks

Golden Portal

Encode your best-practice build once, then diff every client account against it.

Diff primitives exist
New: reference config, practice view
Unlocks
A junior delivers senior-quality work
04 ~4 weeks

Assisted Fix

The tool proposes, a human approves, it executes and logs. Never the reverse.

Cohorts + evidence ready
New: queue, approval, executor
Unlocks
More accounts per head, without more risk
Why this order

Phase 1 first because it is a week of work that converts everything already built into a recurring product. Phase 2 next because permanence beats reporting — a description in HubSpot outlives any PDF. Phase 3 once two clients are on the cycle and variance across the team starts to cost you. Phase 4 last, because writing to a client's production account is only safe once the first three have earned that trust.

How it runs
  1. Only propose from cohorts where the evidence is measured, not inferred.
  2. Start with zero-footprint workflows — provably nothing depends on them.
  3. A human approves every batch. No exceptions, no silent mode.
  4. Log everything. The log is what makes the next batch trustworthy.
What it is worth

Margin. The same team carries more accounts without carrying more risk.

Watch out for

Never let this touch the judgment phases. Automating a decision you have already made is leverage; automating the decision itself is how you break a client's account at scale.

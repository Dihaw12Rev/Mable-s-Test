The benchmark database

Every audit you run already produces the data. Keeping one anonymised row per account costs nothing today and becomes the thing no competitor can copy.

The sentence nobody else in your market can say

“Your workflow hygiene is bottom-quartile for a 15-rep sales org. Here is what the top quartile does differently.”

What you store

Counts, ratios and scores against company size and industry. No names, no records, no content — nothing that identifies a client.

Where it pays

Discovery calls stop being claims and become comparisons. Quarterly reviews get a benchmark. Content writes itself from work already sold.
